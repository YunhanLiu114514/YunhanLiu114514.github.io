01 / Scene and character voice

The room establishes the people through an exchange before naming them. June’s concern appears through a small interruption and a concrete question about dinner.

六台洗衣机排在左墙，右边是折衣桌、兑换机和软木板。柜台后的男人把硬币倒进铁盘，年轻女人攥着一个瘪纸杯。 “别给我换台。”她说。男人收回碰旋钮的手：“我只是调低。楼上有人在看带子。” 她叫朱恩·沃德，是艾琳的妹妹，留在这里等姐姐消息；店主兰德尔替接应者留了楼上的房间。
“我以前没见过你。我看你从楼上下来，猜是她等的人。” “姐姐在找露丝和奥斯卡。公司说人回去了，可露丝儿子问晚饭想吃什么，电话又从‘我这边很好’开始。”

02 / Evidence changes the available dialogue

The player may see that a topic exists before having enough information to ask it. Finding evidence gives returning to a character a purpose.

Observe the room
Find the original recording
Return to June
A new account becomes available

03 / Revision after a playthrough

A name in an action label is information. Removing the premature name preserves discovery while keeping the player’s route clear.

Before

“Go downstairs to find June” reveals an unknown NPC.

After

“Go downstairs” → introduce the shop and people → open conversation.
