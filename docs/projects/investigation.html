Overview

The design goal

Give the player a clear reason to investigate while keeping the uncanny truth discoverable. The main task in Before the Broadcast Ends is to locate two missing people, free them safely and stop the recordings that are using their identities. Dream-related evidence sits underneath that concrete objective.

What I worked on

Original scenario and character development for Before the Broadcast Ends; an option-based adaptation of Crimson Letters; room introductions, scene logic, clue-gated conversations, automatic checks and a playable interface. The project was revised through simulated playtests and direct playthrough feedback.

Before the Broadcast Ends: original scenario. Crimson Letters: single-player adaptation of Alan Bligh’s scenario.

Design decisions

01 / Match actions to what the player knows

A choice should not reveal a character before the scene introduces them. At the opening, the player can go downstairs. June becomes a person to speak to only after entering the shop and hearing the exchange with Randall.

Before

“Go downstairs to find June” reveals an unknown NPC.

After

“Go downstairs” → introduce the shop and people → open conversation.

02 / Let failure change detail, not erase the case

A failed room survey still reveals the basic objects and exits. Success adds useful observations. This lets checks create variation without trapping the story behind one lucky roll.

03 / Make new evidence visible in conversation

June’s recording-identification topic stays unavailable until the player finds the original recording. Returning with evidence opens the topic. Completed actions dim, while repeatable actions remain clearly marked.

Work & evidence

The shipped demo supports independent scenario saves, automatic d100 checks, an evidence notebook and several routes through the investigation. Twenty-nine automated regression checks cover story progression and save behavior across the two scenarios.

Reflection

Simulated and conversational playthroughs exposed unclear goals, premature information and false urgency. The next step is to test with independent players and observe where they lose their sense of purpose, overlook evidence or repeat completed actions.
