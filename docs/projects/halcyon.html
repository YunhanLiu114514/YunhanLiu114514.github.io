Overview

The player should be able to read the consequences of earlier actions through the environment and the people within it. The ending spaces need to communicate a changed world through a combination of layout, detail and NPC dialogue.

What I worked on

My credited contribution is the design and Godot assembly of the bad-outcome world and ending scenes. The game is a team project; the linked repository folder identifies my scene work. The cover image comes from the team playthrough and represents the game as a whole.

Design decisions

01 / Organize the player’s route

Think through where the player enters, which interaction comes into view and when the next piece of information is encountered. This connects narrative order to movement through the scene.

02 / Connect environmental detail and dialogue

Use environmental storytelling alongside NPC dialogue to express consequences. Each element contributes a different kind of information: a place establishes conditions, while a character gives those conditions a human perspective.

03 / Carry the scene into an implemented game

Build and assemble the scenes in Godot, keeping level flow, interaction points and the sequence of information connected to the broader team game.
